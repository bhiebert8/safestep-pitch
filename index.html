Questions people ask first

Will this really hold an adult's full weight?▾
Yes. Every tension pole and tub clamp is load-tested to 300 lbs. The tension mechanism is calibrated: you tighten by hand until the indicator ring turns green, which is the point at which the rated hold is achieved. You cannot under-tighten it and believe it's secure.
Will it mark the ceiling, floor or tile?▾
Nothing is drilled, anchored or permanently adhered. Pressure is spread across wide non-marring pads rather than concentrated at an anchor point, and the strips use a removable adhesive. As with any pressure-mounted product, we recommend checking that your surfaces are sound and following the included setup guide.
Do I need my landlord's permission?▾
SafeStep products install without drilling, anchoring or permanent adhesive, so they generally aren't a structural modification. Lease terms vary, though — every order includes a one-page product information sheet you can share with a landlord or building manager, and we'd suggest checking your lease.
What ceiling heights and tubs does it fit?▾
The pole fits ceilings from 7'2" to 9'4" — about 95% of homes and apartments. The tub clamp fits tub walls 3" to 7" thick, including acrylic, fiberglass, porcelain and tiled surrounds. If yours falls outside that, tell us before you order and we'll say so honestly.
What if it isn't right for us?▾
Send it back within 30 days for a full refund and we cover return shipping. Install it, use it for a month, and decide in the actual bathroom — that's the only test that matters.

Supply relationships

China now, Mexico as the hedge

We have live quotes from a Guangdong manufacturer — 12 years trading, 4.7/5 across 677 reviews. Whether to nearshore turns almost entirely on tariffs.

Mexico is closer than it looks

USMCA is duty-free. A Mexican supplier can charge 29% more than China and still land at the same cost — delivering in a week instead of six. Order-to-shelf drops from ~45 days to ~10, so far less cash sits on a boat.

And it's the tariff hedge

At 0% or 25% Section 301, China wins on landed cost. At 50% or above, Mexico wins outright. Nobody can predict trade policy, so a quoted Mexican alternative is cheap insurance even if we never order.

The plan: start with China because the quotes are real and the supplier base is deep, and get two Mexican quotes in parallel so one policy decision can't strand us. The category isn't widely made in Mexico yet, so this needs real research.

Meta, specifically

How to run the good-son angle without getting blocked

Meta rejects ads that imply we know something about the viewer's health or family. "For your aging parent" gets blocked. But the feeling we're selling is entirely permissible — as long as we describe a scene, never the viewer.

Gets rejected

  • "Worried about your elderly mother?"
  • "Is your dad at risk of falling?"
  • "Caring for an aging parent?"

All three assert something about the person reading it.

Runs fine

  • "She got up. You stayed sitting down."
  • "It took one afternoon."
  • "Her bathroom. Still hers."

All three describe a moment. The viewer decides it's about them.
